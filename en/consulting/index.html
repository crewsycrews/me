What I can help with

Launch or develop a product

I design backends, APIs and databases, build web applications and help with mobile development. I can take a product from its first working version through launch and subsequent improvements.

Outcome: a working product with agreed features and a clear architecture for further development.

Connect systems and reduce manual work

I connect services through APIs and set up data exchange and notifications. I can build an integration with an external provider, an internal service or an intranet portal.

Outcome: automated data exchange with error handling and less duplicate data entry.

Prepare an application for growth

I investigate backend and database bottlenecks, help migrate data and files, automate builds and releases, and select infrastructure for your workload.

Outcome: verified changes, repeatable releases and a migration plan with a rollback option.

Support your engineering team

I help translate business requirements into technical tasks, choose architecture and review code. I can take on complex implementation work and help developers adopt approaches they can maintain independently.

Outcome: agreed technical decisions, prioritised tasks and knowledge shared with the team.

Look after servers, networks and workstations

I handle system administration: Linux and Windows, staff access, local networks, Wi-Fi, VPNs, backups and monitoring. I can also untangle inherited infrastructure.

Outcome: configured infrastructure, documentation and an agreed maintenance process.

Understand what is happening with your IT

I review code, servers and connections between systems to find the causes of failures. I can assess vendor proposals and identify the changes your business actually needs.

Outcome: a clear list of issues and a prioritised action plan.
